Resolution Process

How Civic Complaints Are Resolved

1
1. Submit Complaint

Citizen files complaint with description, exact location, and photo evidence.

2
2. Department Assignment

Municipal administrator reviews and assigns an authorized field technician.

3
3. Field Work

Technician visits site, conducts repairs, and posts progress remarks.

4
4. Verified Resolution

Problem marked as resolved with complete audit log visible to the citizen.
